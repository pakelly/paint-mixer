ΔE reference scale
● < 1 — Indistinguishable. You will not see a difference.
● 1–2 — Barely perceptible under close inspection. Totally fine for mini painting.
● 2–5 — Noticeable side-by-side, but acceptable at tabletop arm's length.
● 5–10 — Clearly off. The hue or value is wrong enough that it looks like a different color.
● 10+ — Wrong color. You're in the ballpark but not the same neighborhood.
For context: a 34-paint collection averages ~ΔE 2.8 across the full line. A 6-paint split-primary palette averages ~ΔE 8.
